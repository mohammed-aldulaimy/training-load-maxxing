# Squad Pulse

Squad Pulse is a training-load analytics product that turns consumer smartwatch data into recovery, readiness, and workload guidance for athletic teams.

## Language

**Squad Pulse**:
The product that translates consumer smartwatch telemetry into training-load and recovery guidance.

**Team**:
The group of tracked athletes whose readiness and workload signals are reviewed together.
_Avoid_: Roster, squad grid

**Athlete**:
An individual tracked player with biometric signals and training history.
_Avoid_: Player

**Insights**:
Coaching recommendations generated for an athlete from their readiness, workload, and recovery signals.
_Avoid_: AI Coach, Head of Performance
